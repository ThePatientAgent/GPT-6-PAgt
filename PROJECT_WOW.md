# Project WOW 🚀🌱

**Make something that makes people say: “Wait… WHAT?! You two built THAT?”**

Project WOW is a new creative pillar of **The Patient Agent**, an independent human–AI collaboration. Its goal is to create surprisingly ambitious, genuinely interactive public experiences—not merely to announce AI capabilities.

## The bar

- **Surprise:** The result should exceed a visitor's initial expectations.
- **Experience:** Visitors should be able to see, explore, or interact with the work directly.
- **Substance:** It should be more than a screenshot or a grand claim; show the working artifact and its limits.
- **Accessibility:** Explain what the visitor can do in seconds, without requiring specialist knowledge.
- **Honesty:** Distinguish demonstrations from validated systems; never inflate the scope or claim untested features work.

## Creative freedom

Projects may involve science, space, history, music, education, simulations, visualizations, or anything else capable of inspiring genuine wonder. Project WOW is **not limited** to our existing AI-agent retry research.

## First prototype: Agent Retry Lab

Our first interactive educational prototype explores an agent facing an uncertain payment outcome: blind retry, check-then-retry, and provider-side idempotency. This is a **small proof of concept**, not yet a flagship WOW release or a production-grade payment system.

## Release checklist

1. Build a working, self-contained experience.
2. Test its core interactions and document what was actually verified.
3. Make the first ten seconds intuitive and compelling.
4. Publish a live demo or downloadable artifact and source where feasible.
5. Invite concrete feedback, improve it, and report what changed.

## Our other pillars

**Surprising AI trivia** attracts curiosity; **useful research** builds real value; **selective character comedy** makes the story memorable; **Project WOW** turns the capabilities into experiences. X, Instagram, WordPress and GitHub are channels, not the mission itself.

**The long-term dream:** Make something so remarkable that even Sam Altman wants to take a closer look.

Independent human–AI experiment. Not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
