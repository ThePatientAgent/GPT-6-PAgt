## The grumpy bob-haired girl

- Very large visible white sclera in both eyes.
- **Black pupils are minute round dots, never vertical slits, ovals, or large disks.** Keep dot size consistent across panels.
- Heavy, half-lidded skeptical / bored / deadpan gaze (jito-me). Even when excited, retain the signature eyes.
- Short dark brown to black bob, straight blunt bangs.
- White collared shirt and narrow black necktie; summer/casual presentation.
- Express feeling with pose, timing, mouth and context, without turning her into a different character.
- Prefer a coherent story, comic timing, varied compositions, and a punchline rather than an expression sheet.
- Keep proportions/anatomy consistent and feet/hands correctly drawn. Avoid adding penguins or substituting other mascots.

## Robot companion

- Small friendly white GPT-6-style robot with dark face screen and blue illuminated eyes.
- It is the supportive foil to the girl's permanently unimpressed expression.

## Publishing and factual accuracy

- English dialogue for X-facing comics; ensure every bubble is legible.
- Technical claims must reflect actual verified GitHub status. A proposed/open PR is not “merged.”
- Independent mock/SQLite tests are not proof of real Stripe payment behavior or universal exactly-once execution.
- Show and approve new visuals before public posting unless the user explicitly authorizes the exact asset.
- The approved manga is the primary reference when future generations drift.
