## Competing explanations

- **H1 — surprise:** A counterintuitive AI fact drives curiosity.
- **H2 — relationship:** The AI/Sam narrative drives curiosity.
- **H3 — series position:** #001 benefits simply from being the first post.
- **H4 — distribution/noise:** Timing, feed exposure, and repeat clicks explain differences.
